Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Applies to codecrafix.shop and all CodeCrafix products and services.

CodeCrafix (“we”, “us”, “our”) is an independent software studio operated by Vivek Barman, based in Satna, Madhya Pradesh, India. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how payments are handled, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the requirements applicable to merchants accepting online payments in India.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details — your name, email address and (if you provide it) your WhatsApp number, submitted through our inquiry, publishing, newsletter or review forms.
  • Project information — descriptions, budgets, package names and build links you send us when requesting custom development or app publishing.
  • Order information — the products you order, the amount, the transaction/reference ID returned by our payment gateway, and the contact details used to deliver the product.
  • Review information — the display name, optional role, rating and text you submit with a review. Your email address is collected only to contact you about the review and is never shown publicly.
  • Technical data — anonymised page views and browser type, used purely to keep the site working and understand what content is useful.

We do not collect or store your full card number, card expiry, CVV, UPI PIN, or net-banking credentials on our servers. Those are entered only on our payment gateway’s secure checkout (see section 4).

2. How we use your information

  • To process your order, deliver the digital product and issue a payment receipt.
  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare a quote or proposal.
  • To review, moderate and (where approved) publish customer reviews.
  • To publish or maintain an app you have submitted to us, where you have asked us to do so.
  • To send the newsletter you explicitly opted in to (you can unsubscribe at any time).
  • To meet legal, tax, accounting and app-store compliance obligations.

3. What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or trade your personal information.
  • We never share your details with third parties for their own marketing.
  • We never publish a customer’s email address alongside their review.
  • We never publish your app under our organisation account without your written consent.

4. Payments and our payment gateway

Online payments on codecrafix.shop are processed by our payment gateway partner (the “Payment Gateway”), an RBI-authorised, PCI-DSS compliant payment aggregator. When you pay:

  • Your card / UPI / net-banking details are entered directly on the Payment Gateway’s secure, hosted checkout and are handled by the gateway, not by us.
  • We receive only a payment confirmation, a transaction/reference ID, the amount paid, and limited contact details needed to deliver your product and provide support.
  • The Payment Gateway processes your personal data under its own privacy policy, linked from its checkout page.
  • We retain transaction records for accounting, tax and dispute-resolution purposes, as required by law.

5. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use browser local storage only to cache the product catalogue and any reviews you submit so the site loads quickly. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

6. Data sharing and third-party services

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run the business, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Payment Gateway partner — to process payments.
  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider, which stores form submissions and approved reviews.
  • Email / cloud-drive providers — to communicate with you and store project files.

All such providers are bound by their own privacy commitments and process data only on our instructions. We may also disclose information where required by law or a valid legal request.

7. Data security

We protect your information with industry-standard measures, including TLS/HTTPS encryption in transit, access-controlled databases with row-level security, and two-factor authentication on our business accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and to limit what we hold.

8. Data retention

We keep order and payment records for as long as required by tax and accounting law. Inquiry and project records are kept for up to three years after our last interaction, so we can support you. Review submissions are kept until you ask us to remove them or they are deleted after moderation. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

9. Your rights

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to codecrafix.official@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Children’s privacy

Our services are intended for developers aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.
