CodeCrafix sells digital goods — downloadable game templates, app source codes, developer tools, prompt packs and similar files — together with custom development and app-publishing services. Because digital products are delivered instantly and cannot be “returned” in the physical sense, a traditional return of goods does not apply.

  • Custom development and publishing/monetisation work are services. A service cannot be returned once work has been delivered or performed.

  • Stopping an in-progress service is governed by our Cancellation Policy.

4. When we will replace instead of return

If you cannot access or use a Product due to a fault on our side, our first remedy is to replace or re-deliver the Product at no cost. This includes:

  • a corrupted or incomplete download;
  • a missing licence key or documentation file;
  • a download link that has expired or stopped working.

Contact us within 7 days of purchase and we will re-deliver or fix the issue promptly.

5. Exclusions

Returns and replacements are not available where:

  • the Product has been used, modified, redistributed or resold in breach of its licence;
  • the request is based on a change of mind after download;
  • the Product is a free item (no payment was made);
  • the issue arises from third-party tools, hardware or platforms outside our control.
