AdMob se paise kaise kamaye (beginner guide)

By Vivek Barman · Updated 5 Oct 2026

Short answer: AdMob aapki app ya game me ads dikhata hai aur har impression ya click par aapko paisa deta hai. Koi guaranteed income nahi hota. Kamai users ki sankhya, unki country, ad format aur app ki quality par depend karti hai.

How AdMob earning works

You add the Google Mobile Ads SDK to your app, create ad units in AdMob, and Google serves ads. Your revenue is roughly the number of ad impressions multiplied by the eCPM (earnings per 1,000 impressions). More active users and better placement mean more impressions, and eCPM varies by country and ad format.

AdMob account se earning tak: step by step

  1. Create an AdMob account with your Google account and add your payment details.
  2. Add your app to AdMob (link it to the Play Store listing once the app is live).
  3. Create ad units: banner, interstitial, rewarded, native or app open.
  4. Integrate the SDK and use Google’s test ads while developing. Never test with your live ads.
  5. Publish the app and declare “contains ads” in Play Console. See our Play Console publishing guide.
  6. Track results in the AdMob dashboard and improve placements.

Which ad format earns more?

  • Rewarded ads: the user opts in for a bonus, so they usually perform well and annoy people less.
  • Interstitial ads: full-screen ads at natural breaks, like between game levels.
  • Banner ads: always visible, easy to add, lower earnings per impression.
  • Native and app open ads: blend with your design or show on launch; use them carefully.

AdMob policy rules jo account bachate hain

  • Never click your own ads or ask others to click. This is invalid traffic and can disable your account.
  • Do not place ads where users can tap them by accident.
  • Do not offer rewards for clicking an ad.
  • Follow the Play Store and AdMob content policies, and keep your privacy policy updated.

AdMob payment kab milta hai?

Google pays once your earnings cross the payment threshold (commonly $100) and your payment profile and address PIN are verified. Check the AdMob Help Center for the current rules in your country.

FAQ

AdMob se kitna paisa kama sakte hain?

There is no guaranteed amount. Earnings depend on your active users, their country, the ad format and the number of impressions.

AdMob payment kab milta hai?

When your earnings pass the payment threshold and your address PIN and payment profile are verified.

Kya apne ads par khud click kar sakte hain?

No. Clicking your own ads, or asking others to, is invalid traffic and can get your AdMob account disabled.

Earnings are not guaranteed. Always follow the official AdMob and Google Play policies.
