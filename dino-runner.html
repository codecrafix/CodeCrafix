About Dino Runner

Dino Runner is a free endless runner inspired by the classic Chrome offline dinosaur game. Run, jump over obstacles and chase a new high score. It is built by CodeCrafix, an independent studio that also builds custom mobile games for clients.

Game features

  • Classic T-Rex endless runner gameplay
  • Dodge obstacles and beat your own high score
  • Endless offline fun
  • Free to download

How to get Dino Runner

  1. Tap Download Now to open the game on the Amazon Appstore.
  2. Install it for free on a supported device. Amazon ended the Appstore on regular Android phones in August 2025, so it is available on Fire tablets and Fire TV.
  3. Open the game and start running.
