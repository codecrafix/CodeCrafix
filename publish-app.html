Free Guides & FAQ

App publish karne se pehle ye jaan lo

Quick answers to the questions developers ask us most. Read the full guides for step-by-step help.

Google Play Console me app free me publish kaise kare?

A Google Play developer account is not free: it needs a one-time $25 fee, and new personal accounts must also run a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days. With our plans you do not have to create the account yourself. Read the full Google Play Console publishing guide.

AdMob se paise kaise kamaye?

Create an AdMob account and ad units, add the Google Mobile Ads SDK to your app, and publish it. Earnings are never guaranteed; they depend on your users and ad impressions. See AdMob se paise kaise kamaye for the full guide, or pick our AdMob Monetisation Plan.

App publish karne ke liye kya chahiye?

An AAB build, app icon, screenshots, a feature graphic, a privacy policy URL, a content rating, the Data safety form and your store listing text. We can prepare most of these for you.

Which apps do you publish?

Only apps that follow Google Play and AdMob policies. We do not publish gambling, betting, lottery, casino or other real-money gaming apps, adult content, or deceptive apps. Every build is reviewed manually, and we may decline or remove an app that breaks the rules.

Mere paas app nahi hai, kya aap bana sakte ho?

Yes. Our custom development service builds Android apps and Unity games, and we can publish them for you afterwards.
