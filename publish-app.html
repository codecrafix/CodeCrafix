Every submission is reviewed manually before publishing. We only publish apps that follow Google Play policy and Indian law.

FAQ

App publish karne se pehle ye jaan lo

Quick answers to the questions developers ask us most.

Google Play Console me app free me publish kaise kare?

A Google Play developer account is not free: it needs a one-time $25 fee, and new personal accounts must also run a closed test with 12 testers for 14 days. With our plans you do not have to create the account yourself.

AdMob se paise kaise kamaye?

Create an AdMob account and ad units, add the Google Mobile Ads SDK to your app, and publish it. Earnings are never guaranteed; they depend on your users and ad impressions — or pick our AdMob Monetisation Plan and we set it all up for you.

App publish karne ke liye kya chahiye?

An AAB build, app icon, screenshots, a feature graphic, a privacy policy URL, a content rating, the Data safety form and your store listing text. We can prepare most of these for you.

Mere paas app nahi hai, kya aap bana sakte ho?

Yes. Our custom development service builds Android apps and Unity games, and we can publish them for you afterwards.
