FAQ

Publish karne se pehle ye jaan lo

Jo sawal har developer humse pehle puchta hai — unke saaf jawab.

Kis type ke apps publish hote hain?
Utility apps, tools, educational apps, casual aur hyper-casual games, productivity aur lifestyle apps — koi bhi original app jo tumhara apna ho ya jiske rights tumhare paas hon.
Kaun se apps publish nahi hote?
Gambling, betting, lottery, casino ya real-money gaming apps; adult content; malware ya spyware; kisi aur developer ke app ki copy ya trademark ka galat istemal; dhokha dene wale (deceptive) apps; aur koi bhi gair-kanooni content. Har submission publish se pehle manually review hota hai.
Kya app ki ownership meri hi rahegi?
Haan, 100%. App aur uska source code hamesha tumhara rahta hai. Hum sirf apne verified organisation console se publish karte hain — tum kabhi bhi plan cancel karke apna app wapas le sakte ho.
Kya AdMob se earning guaranteed hai?
Nahi — aur jo iski guarantee de, usse savdhaan raho. Ad earnings Google AdMob seedha tumhare apne account me deta hai; rakam tumhare users aur ad impressions par nirbhar karti hai. Hum sahi setup aur policy-safe placement ki guarantee dete hain, income ki nahi.
Publish hone me kitna time lagta hai?
Details aur build milne ke baad hum 48 ghante me review karte hain, aur app aam taur par 3 din ke andar Play Store par live ho jata hai. Tum apne dashboard me har step ka status dekh sakte ho.
