Refund Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Applies to all online payments made on codecrafix.shop.

We want you to be confident when you buy from CodeCrafix. Because our catalogue is digital, the rules differ from physical goods — this policy explains exactly when a refund is available, how it is processed, and how long it takes.

1. General principle

Digital products are delivered instantly and cannot be returned, so purchases are generally non-refundable. However, we stand behind what we sell: if something is genuinely wrong on our side, you will be made whole. This policy is written to meet the requirements applicable to merchants accepting online payments in India.

2. Refunds we will approve

You are eligible for a full refund where:

  • Duplicate or double charge — the same order was charged more than once.
  • Erroneous amount — you were charged an amount different from the price shown at checkout.
  • Failed delivery — payment succeeded but the Product was not delivered within 24 hours, despite us being unable to resolve it after you contact us.
  • Not as described / materially defective — the Product does not match its description, is corrupt, or does not function as documented, and we cannot fix or replace it within a reasonable time.
  • Service not delivered — a custom development or publishing service that was paid for but never started, and which you choose to cancel before work begins.

3. Refunds we cannot approve

  • Change of mind after a Product has been downloaded or accessed.
  • The Product works as described but you no longer need it, or lack the skills/tools to use it.
  • Incompatibility caused by your device, tools or platform that was disclosed in the Product requirements.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after purchase for a defect that could reasonably have been found earlier.
  • Free products (no payment was made).
  • Work already completed and delivered for a service engagement.

4. Refund method and timelines

  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method through our payment gateway. We cannot refund to a different card, UPI ID or account.
  • Once approved, we initiate the refund within 2 business days.
  • The payment gateway and your bank then take 5–7 business days (sometimes up to 10 for certain banks or card networks) to credit the amount. This banking timeline is outside our control.
  • For UPI and wallet payments, the credit is usually faster than for cards.
  • You will receive a refund reference/ARN from the payment gateway or your bank that you can use to track the credit.

5. Failed payments and auto-reversals

If a payment fails but an amount is debited by mistake, banks normally auto-reverse it within 5–7 business days. If it is not reversed automatically, contact us with your transaction ID and we will coordinate with our payment gateway to have it reconciled and refunded.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank — almost every issue is resolved faster this way. Raising a chargeback without contacting us first may delay resolution. We provide full cooperation with the payment gateway and the acquiring bank in any dispute.

7. How to request a refund

  1. Email codecrafix.official@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Include your transaction/order ID, the purchased product or service, the date of payment, and the reason with any screenshots.
  3. We acknowledge within 48 hours and confirm the outcome within 7 business days.
