🚀 Publishing Guide

Google Play Console par app kaise publish kare (2026 guide)

By Vivek Barman · Updated 5 Oct 2026

Short answer: Google Play Console me app free me publish nahi hota. Developer account ke liye ek baar $25 lagta hai (yearly nahi). Naye personal account ko production se pehle 12 testers ke saath 14 din ka closed test bhi karna padta hai. Organization accounts ko ye testing rule nahi lagta.

What you need before you start

  • A signed AAB build of your app (Google Play no longer takes new apps as APK).
  • App icon, screenshots, a feature graphic and a short and full description.
  • A public privacy policy URL, a content rating and the Data safety form.
  • A Google account and a one-time $25 registration fee.

Step by step: Play Console par app publish karna

  1. Create a developer account at play.google.com/console, pay the one-time $25 fee and verify your identity.
  2. Create the app in Play Console and choose app or game, free or paid.
  3. Fill the store listing with a keyword-rich title, description, icon and screenshots (this is your ASO).
  4. Complete the policy forms: privacy policy, ads declaration (say yes if you use AdMob), content rating, target audience and Data safety.
  5. Upload your AAB to an internal or closed testing track first.
  6. Run the closed test if you have a new personal account: at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row.
  7. Apply for production access, then submit the release for Google’s review. Once approved, your app goes live.

12 testers, 14 days: kya hai ye rule?

If your personal developer account was created after 13 November 2023, Play Console keeps the Production track locked until a closed test has at least 12 testers who stayed opted in for 14 continuous days. Recruit 20 or more testers, because losing even one tester can break the streak. Organization accounts are exempt from this rule, but they need business verification.

Common reasons first apps get rejected

  • Missing or broken privacy policy link.
  • Data safety answers that do not match what the app really does.
  • Ads in the app but “contains ads” not declared.
  • Not enough active testers for the full 14 days.
  • Outdated target API level.

FAQ

Kya Google Play Console me app free me publish ho sakta hai?

No. A Google Play developer account costs a one-time $25. It is not a yearly fee.

Play Console me 12 testers 14 days ka rule kya hai?

New personal accounts created after 13 November 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 continuous days before applying for production access. Organization accounts are exempt.

Kitna time lagta hai app publish hone me?

On a new personal account, the first app usually takes several weeks because of the 14-day closed test plus Google’s review.

Publish karne ke liye kya kya chahiye?

An AAB build, icon, screenshots, feature graphic, privacy policy URL, content rating, Data safety form and store listing text.

Google changes Play policies often. Check the official Play Console Help for the latest rules before you publish.
