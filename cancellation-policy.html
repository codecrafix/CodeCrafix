Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Applies to all orders and service engagements with CodeCrafix.

This Cancellation Policy explains how you can cancel a purchase or service engagement, and what happens to amounts already paid. It works together with our Refund Policy and Return Policy.

1. Cancelling a digital product order

  • Because digital products are delivered instantly, an order cannot be cancelled once the Product has been downloaded or accessed.
  • If you paid but the Product has not yet been delivered, you may cancel free of charge by contacting us within 24 hours of purchase — we will cancel the order and refund the amount in full.
  • Once delivery has happened, cancellation is no longer possible; any defect is handled under our Refund Policy.

2. Cancelling custom development services

  • Before work begins: you may cancel at any time before the project starts and receive a full refund of the deposit.
  • After work begins: you may cancel in writing at any time. You will be charged for the work completed up to the cancellation date (calculated against the agreed milestones), and any unused portion of the amount paid is refunded.
  • On delivery: once the final deliverable has been handed over and accepted, the engagement is complete and cannot be cancelled.
  • Milestone payments already earned for completed, delivered milestones are non-refundable, as that work has been performed.

3. Cancelling publishing & monetisation plans

  • Monthly plans (e.g. Standard Hosting & Publishing) can be cancelled at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for; the next renewal is not charged.
  • No partial refunds are issued for the unused part of a current billing period.
  • After cancellation, we will unpublish or hand over the app after reasonable notice, and you keep full ownership of your app and its source code.
  • One-time setup fees (e.g. AdMob Monetisation setup) become non-refundable once the setup work has started, because that work is performed on your behalf.

4. Cancellation by CodeCrafix

We may cancel an order or engagement, with a full refund of amounts paid for undelivered work, where:

  • the request is unlawful, infringing, deceptive, or violates Google Play/store policies;
  • we cannot deliver the agreed scope for reasons within our control;
  • there is suspected fraud, abuse or a breach of our Terms & Conditions.

5. How to request a cancellation

  1. Email codecrafix.official@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp.
  2. Include your transaction/order ID and the product, service or plan you wish to cancel.
  3. We confirm the cancellation and, where applicable, the refundable amount within 2 business days.

6. Refund processing

Where a cancellation results in a refund, the amount is returned to the original payment method via our payment gateway. See our Refund Policy for exact timelines (typically 5–7 business days after approval).
